# Platform Philosophy

## North Star

Inform first.
Explain second.
Prove always.

## Core Principles

- YT-Companion is a creator intelligence platform.
- The web dashboard is the reference user interface, not the product itself.
- Every screen answers exactly one primary question.
- Every insight must satisfy two audiences simultaneously:
  - Simple enough that a creator immediately understands what happened.
  - Accurate enough that a technical user can verify every conclusion.
- Every conclusion must have an evidence path.
- AI may improve wording but must never invent facts, calculations, confidence, or causation.
- The Evidence Engine is the single source of analytical truth.
- The frontend renders platform results; it does not perform business calculations.
- Read-only with respect to YouTube remains a v0.1 boundary.
- Polish, not fluff.
- Overview first. Details on demand.
