# Product Vision

## The Product

YT-Companion is not a replacement for YouTube Studio and not merely another analytics dashboard.

It is a self-hosted **creator cockpit**: the place a creator opens first to understand what is happening across their channels, what changed, and what deserves attention.

## Primary Question

The home experience should answer:

> What should I know today?

Raw metrics are supporting evidence, not the entire experience.

## Creator Portfolio

A single installation may contain multiple channels at different stages:

- Active channels receiving regular uploads
- Growing niche channels
- Pre-launch channels waiting for a first video
- Dormant legacy channels
- Archived channels retained for historical reference

The portfolio overview shows cross-channel health and momentum. Selecting a channel opens its dedicated creator cockpit.

## Channel Cockpit

The preferred information order is:

1. Today at a Glance
2. Creator Insights
3. Top Movers
4. Recent Uploads
5. Historical Charts
6. Detailed analytics

This order provides immediate understanding while preserving access to deeper analysis.

## Progressive Disclosure

YT-Companion serves both casual and technical users.

The interface should:

- Use plain language and strong visual hierarchy
- Avoid unexplained abbreviations and metric overload
- Keep advanced detail available rather than removing it
- Allow every insight to expand into its evidence and calculation
- Present warnings when data is incomplete or statistically weak

Easy to read does not mean simplistic.

## Portfolio Insights

Portfolio-level observations may identify:

- The fastest-growing channel
- The channel generating most watch time
- Channels gaining or losing momentum
- Dormant or pre-launch channels that may need attention
- Meaningful differences in audience behavior across channels

Portfolio insights must not compare incompatible metrics without explaining the limitation.

## Creator Insights

Creator Insights convert collected data into defensible observations.

Examples include:

- Family-oriented videos generated more comments per 1,000 views than the channel average.
- Curiosity-based titles produced higher CTR during the selected period.
- Videos published on one weekday showed stronger initial view velocity.

Every insight must provide:

- The observation
- The period analyzed
- The comparison method
- Sample size
- Supporting videos or channel data
- Any important limitation

YT-Companion must never invent statistics or disguise weak evidence as certainty.

## Future Creator Tools

Potential future modules include:

- Channel timelines showing when videos gained momentum
- Thumbnail archives and before/after CTR comparisons
- Title history and performance comparisons
- Video project workspaces containing notes and creative decisions
- Data-driven suggestions for what to make next
- Local or cloud AI providers operating against stored statistics

These are future features and do not change the read-only v0.1 boundary.
